# Dolphin Kanban

Dolphin is a data visualization and document automation platform. Powered by Design Intelligence, it uses JavaScript, Python, LaTeX, and PostScript to transform complex CSV, XLS, XML, and JSON datasets into actionable insights.

## Product Portfolio

1. [`Smart Graphics`](https://desiign.in/demo-dashboard.html) - Vector Art, Logos, Illustrations, Flags, Maps
2. [`Smart Identity`](https://desiign.in/demo-pub-certificate.html) - Certificates, Resumes, Business Cards, Invitations, Letters, Presentations
3. [`Smart Datasets`](https://desiign.in/forms.html) - Intelligent Forms, Data Transformations, Data Visualizations, Data Privacy and Security
4. [`Smart Commerce`](https://desiign.in/demo-pub-branding.html) - Proposals, Quotations, Invoices, Receipts, Work/Purchase Orders, Customs EXIM (Export/Import) Documentation
5. [`Smart Contract`](https://desiign.in/) - Legal Contract Paperwork, NDAs, MOUs, BAAs, IPRs, EULAs, SLAs
6. [`Smart Business`](https://desiign.in/demo-pub-doc-automation.html) - 1 to 5 + Reports, Books, Manuals, Performance Dashboards, Infographics
7. [`Smart Hospital`](https://desiign.in/demo-health.html) - 1 to 6 + Prescriptions and other AI-ready Electronic Health Records
8. [`Smart Academia`](https://desiign.in/demo-education.html) - 1 to 6 + School and University Documentation and Information Management System
